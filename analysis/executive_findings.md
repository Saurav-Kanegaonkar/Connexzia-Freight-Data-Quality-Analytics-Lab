# Executive Findings

The generated scorecard identifies carrier-level variation in on-time delivery, enabling a narrow review of lanes and carriers below the illustrative 90% threshold. The refresh output shows that failed or late runs often co-occur with higher exceptions, so refresh status alone is not a sufficient control.

Recommended operating cadence: triage missing ETA/POD fields before reporting cutoff, investigate low-performing carrier-lane combinations weekly, and page the data/reporting owner for failed or >60-minute runs. These are scenario findings, not claims about Connexzia performance.
